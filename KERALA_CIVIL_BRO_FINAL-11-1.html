05 · Important Rule Notes

Before fixing your house plan

  • Front yard is 3.00 m average and 1.80 m minimum for the general Table 4 A1 case; 1.80 m does not mean 1.80 m can simply be used everywhere.
  • If the plot is ≤125 m² and built-up area ≤200 m², the small-plot values can apply subject to the rule conditions.
  • For buildings up to 10 m in height, one side and/or rear yard may be reduced up to 0.50 m only where there is no opening on that portion.
  • For Group A1/A2/F buildings with built-up area ≤200 m² and height ≤8 m, a side/rear yard may in specified cases be reduced below 0.50 m or abut the boundary other than a street boundary, with written consent of the adjoining land owner and no opening on that portion.
  • Neighbour consent alone does not automatically allow 0 m setback. The exact special provision and all conditions must be satisfied.
  • Buildings above 10 m height get an additional minimum-yard requirement of 0.50 m for every 3 m or fraction above 10 m; other Rule 26 exceptions and special provisions may also apply.
  • Road classification, town-planning schemes, Fire & Rescue/NOC, CRZ and other applicable provisions can change the result.
Calculation audit

Engineering Calculation Notes

  • Quantity calculators are estimation/take-off tools unless the result explicitly says otherwise.
  • IS 456 span/depth screen uses Clause 23.2.1 basic ratios and the >10 m span correction; steel modification factors are intentionally not guessed.
  • Development length uses the IS 456 bond-stress expression as a base calculation; applicable bar/concrete grade, bond condition and detailing must be selected by the engineer.
  • Column and footing steel outputs are quantity estimates, not reinforcement design. Anchorage, laps, seismic detailing and structural analysis are outside these calculators.
  • KPBR screening is based on the 2019 rules and the 2025 amendments; it is not an automated permit approval.
