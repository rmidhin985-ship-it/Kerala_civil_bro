Add Estimate Item

House estimate items include foundation, RCC structure, masonry, finishes, doors/windows, roofing, waterproofing, plumbing, electrical, septic tank and water tank. Plumbing & sanitary and electrical works include dedicated fixture/point inputs; their overall rate can be entered for BOQ costing.

Nominal proportions for material estimating. Use the project specification; this selection does not approve an RCC design mix.

For Footing / Column / Beam / Plinth Beam / Lintel / Sunshade, enter reinforcement exactly from the structural drawing. Steel is estimated from the entered bar details; this tool does not design reinforcement. If tie length is zero, only main bars + extra steel are counted. Column ties are spaced over Height; beam/lintel ties over Length. The same detailing applies to each measurement line; use separate items for different detailing.

Enter slab reinforcement exactly as shown in the structural drawing. Main bars are calculated along Length and spaced across Width; distribution bars run along Width and are spaced across Length. This tool estimates quantity only and does not design reinforcement.

Plumbing & Sanitary Details

Enter fixture counts and approximate pipe lengths from the plumbing layout/site requirement. Rate can be entered as the overall plumbing & sanitary rate; the entered details are shown in the estimate description.

Electrical Details

Enter electrical point counts from the electrical layout/site requirement. Rate can be entered as the overall electrical work rate; point details are retained in the estimate description.

Painting Material Details

Coverage depends on product and surface. Enter the manufacturer/site values where available. Wastage % is applied to Putty, Primer and Paint.

Tank Component Quantities

Enter component quantities from the approved drawing / site estimate. The software does not design septic or water tanks. Concrete materials use the selected concrete mix; masonry/plaster materials use the separate specification inputs below.

Custom size is the actual Brick / Solid Block / Laterite Stone size. Enter Length × Width × Height in mm.

Reinforcement quantity (from drawing)

Steel here is a quantity take-off, not reinforcement design. Use a bar schedule for different directions, layers, curtailment, laps and hooks. Do not add the same steel twice in a separate item. Enter a composite rate only if it includes the intended steel work.

Bar groups — entire work item

Each group is one diameter, cutting length and total bar count for the ENTIRE item, including all Nos, flights, landings and measurement lines. Add separate groups for both directions/layers. Include drawing-specified anchorage, hooks and laps in cutting lengths. This schedule is not multiplied by Nos again.

Staircase geometry

Straight flights only. Rise = riser count × riser height; run = tread count × tread depth. Waist thickness is normal to the slope. Step blocks = ½ × riser × tread × width × tread count. Enter landings separately, without overlapping flight volumes. Use direct quantity for curved, spiral, tapered or other geometry.

Plaster specification

Material quantities use this thickness and the selected mortar ratio. BOQ quantity remains the net plaster area.

Masonry assumptions

Size is the actual unit size. Count uses (L + joint) × (W + joint) × (H + joint). Wet mortar is the difference between masonry volume and solid unit volume. Verify bond, cuts, openings and actual joint arrangement; this is a volume-based estimate.

RR material coefficients

Supply-volume coefficient includes assumed stone packing. Set both coefficients to match the specification and site practice; they are not solid-volume fractions to be added together.

Tank masonry / plaster specification

Component quantities above are TOTALS for one complete tank item. Selected concrete mix applies only to RCC components. Set masonry and plaster mortar ratios here; reinforcement is the entered total kg. Add waterproofing and accessories through additional materials if required.

Finishing material details

Area remains the BOQ basis. Enter tile/slab dimensions for piece count; include cutting allowance in wastage. Bedding, adhesive and grout are optional and depend on the selected specification.

DPC specification

For concrete DPC, materials use this thickness and the selected concrete mix. BOQ is measured in m².

Waterproofing product

Use manufacturer coverage for the actual surface. Add membrane, primer, reinforcement mesh or other components below if required.

Roof sheet coverage

Enter effective dimensions AFTER laps. Piece count is an area-based allowance, not a roof-layout cutting plan. Enter steel, screws, ridges, flashing and other accessories below.

Service costing

Overall rate is the amount for the entire item. In item-wise mode, every entered fixture/point/pipe quantity is multiplied by its own rate, plus priced additional-material rows. Zero rates are allowed and shown as unpriced; no market rates are assumed.

Additional materials / components (optional)

Enter totals for this work item: e.g. door frame, shutter, glass, hardware; roof steel/accessories; electrical wires, conduit and protection; plumbing fittings. These are added to auto-calculated materials, so do not repeat them. Wastage is applied only when selected per row. Unit rates affect the BOQ only in service item-wise costing mode.

Additional Measurement Lines

Enter the first measurement above. Add more lines for the same work item; their quantities are summed. Direct Quantity overrides all lines.

Deductions / കുറയ്ക്കേണ്ട അളവുകൾ

Enter openings separately for each work item. Dimensions are in metres. Direct Quantity is treated as gross; deductions are then subtracted. If it is already net, leave deductions empty. No automatic measurement-standard exemptions are applied; enter only the deductions applicable to your specification. For plaster/paint, enter the number of faces in Nos. RCC deductions do not reduce drawing-based steel automatically.
